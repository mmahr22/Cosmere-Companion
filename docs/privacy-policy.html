Privacy Policy — Cosmere Companion

Last updated: 2026-08-26

Cosmere Companion is an offline companion app for the Cosmere RPG tabletop game. This policy explains what the app does — and does not — do with your data.

Data collection

Cosmere Companion does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission and makes no network requests of any kind — there are no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party services integrated into the app.

Data storage

All data you create in the app — characters, their stats, inventory, notes, and any photo you choose to attach as a character portrait — is stored only in a local database and local file storage on your own device. This data is never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

If you use the app's character export feature, the exported file is written to your device and shared only through the destination you explicitly pick (e.g. Files, Drive, email) via Android's standard share sheet — the app itself does not send it anywhere.

Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions. Character portraits are chosen through the Android system photo picker, which does not require the app to be granted broad photo-library access.

Children's privacy

Since the app collects no data of any kind from any user, it does not knowingly collect data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted at this same URL with a revised "last updated" date.
