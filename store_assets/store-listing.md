## Full description (max 4000 chars)

Cosmere Companion is a free, offline companion app for the Cosmere RPG
(Stormlight Handbook) by Brotherwise Games — built for players who want a
faster character sheet and dice tray at the table.

FEATURES

Characters
- Guided character creation: ancestry, culture, attributes, heroic path and
  specialty, skills, and (for Radiant characters) bonding and surges.
- An interactive character sheet with health, focus, and investiture
  trackers, computed defenses, editable skills and expertises, and inline
  dice rolling right from each skill or weapon.
- Level-up flow with a proper points budget for attributes, skills, and
  talents, plus GM bonus points for ad-hoc rewards.
- Talent purchase tracking, Radiant Ideal progression, and support for
  taking on additional heroic paths or swearing later Ideals through play.
- Inventory with carrying capacity and weapon-wielding limits, equip/unequip,
  and live combat stats for equipped weapons and armor.
- Support for a full roster of characters, with export/import so you can
  back up a character or hand them to another device.
- Optional character portraits.

Dice
- Skill test rolls with advantage/disadvantage and the Plot Die
  (Opportunities and Complications), plus weapon damage rolls — all inline
  wherever they come up on the sheet.

Reference
- A searchable, categorized rules browser covering ancestries, cultures,
  heroic and Radiant paths, talents, Singer forms, surges, items, and
  conditions — with tappable cross-links so you can jump from a character's
  sheet straight to the relevant rule and back.

No account, no internet connection, and no ads — everything is stored
locally on your device. You'll need your own copy of the Stormlight Handbook
to play; this app is a tool for the table, not a replacement for the book.

This is unofficial fan content, created and shared for non-commercial use.
It has not been reviewed by Dragonsteel Entertainment, LLC or Brotherwise
Games, LLC, and it contains no book text.
